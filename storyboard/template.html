Story

The Finder

A psychic girl is held in an institute's white room. Her gift is getting lost: she drifts out of herself, and the white coats who chart her can't find where she goes. So they bring in a man whose gift is finding people. He finds her, and he finds something in her that he wasn't sent for. He offers her three things he can help her find: protecting, trusting, believing. On screen these become three threads of light between them.

The institute decides she isn't worth the time. It hands him a gun and a single bullet and sends him to find her. He finds her and doesn't shoot. They run through tunnels, motels, diners and alleys until the coats come round the bend. The coats cut his three threads and leave him the gun and the bullet. They take her senses one by one and leave her the years.

The song opens and closes on the same verse, so the video is a loop. We start in the white room and end there years later, with her replaying the agains. Whether his voice at the end is a memory or a return stays open.

This grew from your Mage: The Ascension premise, with the agent and the psychic girl taken for experimentation. The characters, the institute and its circle-and-crosshair mark are all original designs, with no game iconography.

Look

Clinical ink, sodium light

Everything is drawn as line. Institute scenes are overlit paper with a graph grid, black ink and one red pen. Memory scenes invert to night navy, where he is a single warm contour and she is light made of points. The run is the only place colour gets warm and saturated. There is film grain throughout, and bloom only on light sources.

This follows the track's dark-synthpop split: cold, clinical textures against breathy, reverb-lit vocals, with nursery-rhyme chants in between.
